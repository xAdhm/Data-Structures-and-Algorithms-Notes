# Graphs - Overview

A **graph** is any collection of nodes and connections between them — nodes are also called **vertices**, connections are called **edges** (same terminology introduced in [[Trees and Graphs - Nodes, Vertices, and Edges]]).

## Trees are graphs, with restrictions
A [[Binary Trees - Overview|binary tree]] *is* a graph — just a heavily restricted one: every node has at most one parent and at most two children. Lifting these restrictions opens up far more graph shapes than trees allow.

## Graphs are everywhere
Social networks (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok), road networks, circuit boards, internet traffic, blockchains, biological models — nearly anything can be modeled as a graph.
